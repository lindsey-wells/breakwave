# BW-SUPPORT-01C Structure Contract

BW-SUPPORT-01C reorganizes the Support tab around what the user is trying to do rather than giving every category equal visual weight.

## Locked behavior

- Get help now remains first and expanded by default.
- Seven task-based groups replace nine similarly weighted groups.
- The group order is: immediate help, recovery setup, learning, privacy, Plus, about/contact, and more tools.
- Teach Me How to Use BreakWave belongs first in the learning group.
- The learning group has the stable key `support-learn-breakwave-group` for BW-ONBOARD-01B.
- Who We Are leads the combined About and contact group.
- More tools remains last because data export is a less-frequent action.
- No emergency, privacy, contact, learning, Plus, or export function is removed.
- Rescue and immediate-danger guidance remain available regardless of setup, recovery mode, or paid status.

## Visual intent

Collapsed groups use one clear title, one short description, and a compact icon treatment. The removed eyebrow/title duplication reduces scrolling while preserving readable wrapping and accessible tap targets.
