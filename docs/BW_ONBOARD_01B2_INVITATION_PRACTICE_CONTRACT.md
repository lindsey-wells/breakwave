# BW-ONBOARD-01B2 — Invitation and Practice Contract

## Goal

Help a newly onboarded user discover the replayable Teach Me BreakWave tour and safely practice the core Rescue decision flow without changing any recovery record or launching an external action.

## Post-onboarding invitation

- The invitation appears only after a user completes onboarding.
- Skipping onboarding does not show the invitation.
- Established users are not retroactively interrupted by it.
- The invitation offers **Show me** and **I'll explore myself**.
- Declining never removes the permanent Support tutorial entry.
- The user's choice is stored locally so the invitation is not repeatedly offered.
- When the user selected Review BreakWave Plus during onboarding, the existing Plus information handoff opens first. The tutorial invitation appears only after the Plus screen closes.
- The invitation never changes entitlement, purchase, subscription, or access state.

## Practice Rescue sandbox

Practice Rescue must remain visibly labeled as practice and no-save throughout the experience.

Practice Rescue may read the selected recovery mode and saved Personal Why for display only. It must not:

- create or edit a Log entry;
- change insights, streaks, progress, plans, onboarding, tutorial completion, or recovery data;
- edit or save Personal Why;
- open messaging, calling, emergency, Support, or other external actions;
- grant Plus or alter entitlement;
- block real Rescue.

Practice Rescue teaches:

- urge intensity selection;
- read-only Personal Why recall;
- the Calm Reset breathing tool;
- selection of one next right action.

Selecting **Text someone safe** in practice shows a no-message confirmation and never opens the messaging app. Android Back and **Exit practice** leave immediately. **Finish practice** returns to the tutorial and records nothing.

## Regression boundary

BW-ONBOARD-01B2 does not change the real Rescue completion path, Log repository, emergency support actions, billing, or the approved Free-versus-Plus policy.
